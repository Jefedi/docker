# Roller Shutter Parts Reference (France)

## Sortie de Caisson (Crank Output / Gearbox Output)

The part that connects the crank handle (manivelle) to the winding mechanism (treuil) inside the shutter box.

### Key Specifications to Ask For

| Spec | Common Values |
|------|--------------|
| Angle | 45° (façade/sous-coffre), 90° (façade), 60°, 120° |
| Tige côté treuil | Carré 6mm, **Carré 8mm** (most common), Hexagonal 7mm (6P7), Rond |
| Entrée côté manivelle | Cardan femelle Ø12mm (most common), Mâle Ø12mm |
| Platine (fixation) | Métal ou plastique, 2 or 4 trous, various entraxes |
| Matière | Acier zingué/nickelé, plastique blanc/gris, aluminium |
| Couleur | Blanc, gris, argent/nickel, brun sépia, noir |

### Typical Prices
- Sortie de caisson standard: 10-30€

## Lock Cylinders for Shutters vs Doors

### Roller Shutter Locks (Verrous à Pompe)
- Use pump key system (clé à pompe tubulaire)
- Rectangular mounting plate with 2 screw holes
- Short cylindrical body
- Mounted on shutter frame or bottom rail

### Pump Cylinders for Front Doors (Muel/Vak)
- Same pump key system but different mounting
- Often round plate with 6 holes or specific Muel/Vak pattern
- 8 gorges 1 pompe configuration
- Replacement: Cylindre adaptable Muel-Vak CYC ~250€

### Fichet 787
- Monobloc with 2 ears for screws
- Pump/tubular key
- Specific to Fichet blindé doors
- Replacement: ~210-900€ depending on model
