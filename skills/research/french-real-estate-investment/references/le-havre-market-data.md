## Caravelle Cormorans Pattern (Recurring)

The Résidence Caravelle Cormorans in Caucriauville is a **recurring source** of sub-75k€ listings. Properties here share:

- **Price**: 59k-75k€ for 53-84m²
- **DPE**: Currently D, transitioning to A-C (BBC renovation)
- **Travaux voted**: ~8 900€ mandatory per unit (isolation, fenêtres, VMC, raccordement BBC)
- **Reste à charge after subventions**: ~7 800€
- **Copropriété**: ~110 lots, with ascenseur, espaces verts, parking collectif
- **Tramway**: Proximity to tram lines

**Why it's attractive despite travaux:**
- Post-renovation DPE A-C means no future rental restrictions
- Energy savings for tenant = higher achievable rent
- Value bump post-renovation (est. +15-25%)
- Very low purchase price/m² (~850-1100€/m²)
- Cashflow positive even after factoring 9k€ travaux

## Vendu Loué vs Libre — Typical Yields

| Type | Typical gross yield | Typical net yield |
|---|---|---|
| Vendu loué (small studio, student area) | 7-10% | 5-6.5% |
| Vendu loué (T2/T3, family area) | 8-9.5% | 4.5-6% |
| Libre (small studio) | 7-8.5% | 4.5-5.5% |
| Libre (T3/T4, large surface) | 10-18% | 6-11% |

**Note:** Large-surface "libre" properties show inflated yields because the rental estimate is speculative. Smaller "vendu loué" yields are more conservative because the real rent is typically below market.
